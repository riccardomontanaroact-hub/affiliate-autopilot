# VORLI — Affiliate disclosure research (16 October 2026)

Status: internal research draft. No production release.

The Italian advertising self-regulatory body IAP states that affiliate marketing content should visibly disclose its promotional purpose. Its 2024 update also addresses affiliate hashtags for social posts.
Sources:
- https://www.iap.it/notizie/nuove-regole-influencer/
- https://www.iap.it/notizie/nuovo-regolamento-digital-chart/

AGCOM published updated influencer transparency FAQs in March 2026. Their applicability to a particular publisher requires an individual assessment.
Source: https://www.agcom.it/influencer-linee-guida-codice-di-condotta-e-faq

Awin's publisher FAQ includes both a free-signup statement and a description of a one-time verification deposit; its content-creator page also advertises free signup. Do not assume a new signup has zero upfront cost without checking the applicable flow.
Sources:
- https://www.awin.com/it/faq
- https://www.awin.com/it/pricing/content-creators

eWeki IT public Awin listing indicates a 30-day attribution window; personalized commission and acceptance status are not verified.
Source: https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/19988
