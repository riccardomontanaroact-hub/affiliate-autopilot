# VORLI — Awin publisher pricing review (21 October 2026)

Status: research only. Not a sales offer or legal advice.

## Official-source discrepancy

Awin's Italian affiliate-partner pricing page describes publisher registration as free with no upfront costs: https://www.awin.com/it/pricing/affiliate-partners

The Italian FAQ also states that a one-time identity-verification deposit is required and reimbursed after reaching the first payment threshold: https://www.awin.com/it/faq

These public statements appear inconsistent. Do not assume either condition applies to a specific existing account without reviewing its current terms. Do not pay or open another account.

## Consumer and editorial checks

EU consumer guidance describes a general 14-day withdrawal period for distance purchases, with a conditional exception for digital content where the buyer expressly consents to immediate performance and acknowledges losing withdrawal rights: https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_it.htm

A digital-product checkout should not be activated before confirming Italian implementation, required information, consumer consent, and seller tax obligations with qualified advice.
