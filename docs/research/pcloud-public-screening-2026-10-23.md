# pCloud — public affiliate screening (2026-10-23)

Official sources:
- https://www.pcloud.com/affiliate_terms.html
- https://www.pcloud.com/it/affiliate.html
- https://help.pcloud.com/it/article/affiliate-program

Public terms: no registration fee, age 18+, 20% eligible commissions, $10 PayPal payout minimum, $500 bank-transfer minimum, and monthly payouts no sooner than 45 days after a qualifying referral. The terms and FAQ specify a 45-day cookie, but the landing-page graphic claims 90-day tracking. Do not rely on the 90-day claim without clarification.

No VORLI enrollment, approval, earnings, or payments have been verified. No referral links included. Individual eligibility and Italian tax obligations remain unverified. This is research only; no publication or financial action is authorized.
