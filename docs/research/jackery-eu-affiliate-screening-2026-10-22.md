## Editorial draft: How to estimate portable power station autonomy
Watts (W) measure power, while watt-hours (Wh) measure energy. Dividing nominal Wh by a constant load in W yields a theoretical runtime, not a guarantee. For example, 500 Wh divided by 50 W is 10 theoretical hours, before losses. Actual runtime varies with conversion efficiency, standby consumption and load changes.
Before selecting equipment, verify continuous output, peak demand, supported connectors, weight, warranty and solar input compatibility in the official manuals. Do not assume portable batteries are suitable substitutes for critical backup systems.
