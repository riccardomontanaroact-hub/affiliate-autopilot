# VORLI - Commercial publication checklist (draft)
Research date: 2026-10-16. Not approved for production.

1. Verify each advertiser's current approval, market and terms in the network dashboard. Emails and public commission pages alone are insufficient.
2. Confirm product specifications, seller, warranty, returns, and source dates before writing recommendations.
3. Clearly label commercial relationships before any affiliate link. Google recommends rel=sponsored on affiliate links.
4. Do not claim product tests, prices, discounts, commissions or earnings without evidence.
5. Direct sales of digital downloads require a separate review of consumer withdrawal rules, tax treatment and seller obligations.
6. Keep private account information and tracking URLs out of the public repository.
7. Human review is required before production deployment, payments, or sending messages.
