# Standby energy: research note (2026-10-14)

EU rules for standby and networked standby apply to relevant newly marketed equipment since 9 May 2025. Source: https://energy.ec.europa.eu/news/eu-consumers-save-energy-new-limits-standby-modes-electrical-appliances-2025-05-08_en

For educational estimates: annual kWh = watts × hours/day × days/year / 1000. This is not a promise of savings. Draft only; do not publish.
