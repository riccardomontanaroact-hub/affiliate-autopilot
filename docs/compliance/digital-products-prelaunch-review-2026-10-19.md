# VORLI — pre-launch compliance review: digital downloads and affiliate disclosure

Research date: 2026-10-19. Status: research and editorial checklist only. Not legal or tax advice; not approved for production.

## Digital guides / downloadable files
- Do not assume that all digital downloads are automatically non-refundable. Under EU consumer-rights rules, the exception to the 14-day withdrawal right for non-tangible digital content requires specific conditions, including the consumer's prior express consent to start delivery during the withdrawal period, acknowledgement of loss of withdrawal right, and contractual confirmation.
- The European Commission's guidance says a pre-ticked checkbox or blanket acceptance of terms is not enough for express consent and acknowledgement. A user must take a positive action. Review the final Italian consumer-facing flow and record the required confirmation before activating checkout.
- Separate the right of withdrawal from statutory remedies for digital content that is not supplied or does not conform to the contract. Avoid a blanket “no refunds” statement.
- Do not turn on payments or immediate digital delivery until a qualified professional has reviewed the checkout, seller identification, pre-contract disclosures and applicable taxes.

## Affiliate transparency
- Show a prominent, understandable disclosure near affiliate recommendations, not solely in a footer. Label paid promotional content and do not imply independent reviews of products that were not tested.
- Do not claim guaranteed income, commissions, approval or live discounts without verification.
- Advertiser approval must be checked in the network dashboard. Promotional messages and generic welcomes do not by themselves prove current active status.

## Italian seller / VAT checks before launch
- Obtain an individualized assessment of whether planned recurring affiliate and direct digital sales constitute an ongoing business activity requiring VAT registration and which invoicing, consumer and cross-border VAT rules apply. Do not assume that operating as an individual automatically exempts the seller.
- Do not collect payments until this assessment is completed.
